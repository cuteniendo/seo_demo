Welding School With Housing: What $17,050 All-In Actually Buys You

By Tyler Sasse, Founder & CEO — 20 years as a working welder before founding Western Welding Academy in 2019

Yes — welding schools with housing exist, and Western Welding Academy is one of them. Every program's tuition includes furnished housing five minutes from campus, utilities and WiFi covered, for the full length of the program. Here's exactly what that means in dollars, what the fine print says, and who it's not actually for.

Bottom line: housing, tools, PPE, and materials are already inside the tuition number — $17,050 for Foundational, up to $35,800 for Expert. That's $3,000 to $6,000 you don't have to go find on top of it, minus a refundable $500 deposit we'd rather tell you about now than let you find on an invoice.

Does Welding School Provide Housing? What "All-In" Actually Includes

Furnished apartment, five minutes from campus. Here's what's covered, and what's on you:

  • WiFi, utilities, and parking — covered
  • Furnished, twin bed provided
  • Linens, toiletries, and food — you bring these
  • Roommate matching available on request

Pack like you're moving somewhere for three to six months, because you are. Most schools call it "student housing" and let the specifics stay vague until you've already paid a deposit somewhere else to hold your spot. Here, it's one line on one invoice.

Most "Housing Assistance" Isn't Housing

Ask a few welding schools what "housing assistance" actually means and watch the answer get thinner the longer they talk. At more than one school we looked at, it's a phone number and a list of hotels they've negotiated a discount with — a suggestion, not housing. Compare that to a line item that's already inside your tuition and doesn't require you to make a single call.

Welding School Room and Board Cost — The Real Comparison

Housing alone represents $3,000 to $6,000 in savings compared to a program that doesn't include it. Run the math against community college instead: tuition runs $3,000 to $10,000, plus $1,500 to $3,000 for tools and materials that aren't included, plus whatever an apartment costs in that town for the length of the program. None of that shows up on the sticker price.

For the price of one semester of a four-year degree's general-ed requirements, you could be fully certified and working. That's not a slogan — a 12-week Foundational Pipe Welder program is $17,050 all-in, and entry salary starts at $40,000. For the national picture on where that leads, the Bureau of Labor Statistics' outlook for welders is a reasonable place to start.

The Fine Print — Deposit, Rules, and What to Pack

The all-in number isn't the only number. Here's the rest of it:

  • Refundable $500 security deposit, separate from tuition
  • Included for every student except those married with children — you arrange your own place near campus in that case
  • No pets
  • Roommate requests go in with your application, matched where possible

The bed's provided. Everything else — sheets, the good pillow, your actual life — is on you to pack.

Moving Away for Welding School — What to Expect

Forty hours a week, 85% of it booth-side on real pipe and structural steel — not a classroom, not a simulator. That's the actual schedule, and it's a full-time job before it's a paycheck. Nobody's grading you on attendance. You're either in the booth or you're behind.

There's a specific kind of tired that comes from a 40-hour week with a hood on. It's a good tired. It's also just tired. Every instructor running that booth has done the job in the field first — the program is built around real pipe and structural welding, backed by an AWS Accredited Testing Facility standard, not a generic curriculum.

Who This Isn't For

If you're married with kids and already have a place lined up near Gillette, the housing math in this post isn't about you — you're arranging your own, and that's expected going in. And if you're shopping purely on sticker price, a $500 weekend workshop will always beat us there. It just won't get you hired. Compare it against the full program lineup before you decide what actually matters to you.

The Housing Questions We Get Every Week

Is housing mandatory at Western Welding Academy?

Yes, unless you're married with children — then you're expected to arrange your own housing nearby. Everyone else lives in the housing that's already included in tuition.

Is there a security deposit?

$500, refundable. It's the one number the all-in tuition figure doesn't cover, and we'd rather say so here than have you find it on an invoice.

What's furnished, and what do I need to bring?

A twin bed and the basics. Linens, toiletries, and food are on you — pack like you're actually moving, because you are.

Can I request a roommate?

Yes. Put the request in with your application and we'll match it where we can.

Can my spouse or kids stay with me?

No. The included housing is single-occupancy per student. Married students with children arrange their own housing near campus instead.
