A closer look

What each program actually includes

12 weeks / $17,050 all-in

Foundational Pipe Welder

SMAW from day one, booth-side, on real pipe. This is the shortest, least expensive path to a field-ready certification — built for someone who wants to be working, not studying, inside three months.

  • 2 certifications, entry salary $40,000–$60,000
  • Furnished housing, tools, and PPE included
  • Most grads clear tuition in 5–7 months of pay
19 weeks / $27,600 all-in

Professional Pipe Welder

SMAW plus GTAW. Two processes means two more doors open on a job posting board — GTAW especially is what separates a stick-only résumé from one that clears a harder employer test.

  • 4 certifications, entry salary $50,000–$90,000
  • Same all-in tuition structure, seven weeks longer
  • Lifetime retraining included
24 weeks / $35,800 all-in

Expert Pipe Welder

The full 17-certification stack. This is the program behind our highest entry salaries — $60,000 to $125,000+ — and most Expert-track grads clear their tuition in under five months once they're working.

  • 17 certifications — the widest range we offer
  • Top 10% of all graduates earn $125,000+ annually
  • Same housing, tools, and PPE included
